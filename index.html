About Me

For more than 20 years, I have helped industrial manufacturers build and expand their international business across EMEA, CIS, and global markets. My work has included market expansion strategy, international business development, distributor network development, strategic sourcing, supply chain development, commercial negotiations, export process design, and leading cross-functional teams, all with the objective of creating long-term international growth.

I believe successful international business is not built on occasional export orders or isolated purchasing opportunities. It requires well-defined market strategies, trusted commercial partnerships, effective sourcing and supply structures, disciplined operational processes, and sound risk management. Throughout my career, my role has been to help companies transform international ambitions into structured, scalable, and sustainable operations.

Working across diverse markets has also shown me that international business is fundamentally about people. Conducting business across different languages and cultures has deepened my appreciation of the trust, communication, and cross-cultural understanding required to build lasting international partnerships.

Alongside my international business experience, I have developed a software development background that strengthens my ability to work with engineering-driven products and technology companies. This technical foundation has expanded my interest in advanced manufacturing sectors including automation, robotics, AI-enabled systems, EV components, IoT, and clean technology.

Today, based in China, I work at the intersection of international business, advanced manufacturing, technology, and global supply chains, with a particular focus on opportunities connecting China with international markets.

Whether the challenge is entering a new market, developing international business, building commercial partnerships, establishing strategic sourcing, or creating a more structured approach to cross-border growth, I am open to exploring how I can contribute.

How I Work

I work with companies that want to grow internationally, build stronger cross-border operations, or make better use of opportunities between China and international markets.

Depending on the situation, I can work as an independent business development partner, strategic advisor, project-based consultant, or external resource for a specific market or commercial initiative.

Market Expansion & International Business Development

I help companies identify, evaluate, and develop international market opportunities.

This can include market-entry strategy, partner and distributor development, international business development, export strategy, commercial negotiations, market research, and building the relationships and processes required to turn international opportunities into sustainable business.

While much of my experience has been helping companies expand from Europe and other markets internationally, my current base in China also creates opportunities to support companies looking to understand, enter, develop, or build relationships within the Chinese market.

Strategic Sourcing & Supply Chain Development

I help companies develop more effective international sourcing and supply structures.

This can include identifying and evaluating suppliers, developing sourcing strategies, comparing manufacturing alternatives, negotiating with suppliers, assessing commercial and operational risks, and designing supply chains that support quality, cost-efficiency, reliability, and long-term growth.

Being based in China gives me a practical perspective on one of the world's most important manufacturing ecosystems and its connection to international markets.

Consulting, Training & Speaking

I also work on a project or knowledge-sharing basis.

This can include strategic consulting, workshops, export and international business training, executive discussions, guest lectures, conference presentations, and speaking engagements.

My approach is practical and experience-based: turning real international business challenges into clear frameworks, decisions, and actions.
