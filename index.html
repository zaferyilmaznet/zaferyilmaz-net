About Me

For more than 20 years, I have helped industrial manufacturers build and expand their international business across EMEA and CIS markets. My work has included market expansion strategy, international business development, distributor network development, commercial negotiations, export process design, and leading cross-functional teams — all with the objective of creating long-term international growth.

I believe successful international expansion is not built on isolated sales opportunities or occasional export orders. It requires well-defined market strategies, trusted commercial partnerships, disciplined operational processes, and effective risk management. Throughout my career, my role has been to help companies transform international ambitions into structured, scalable, and sustainable international operations.

Working across diverse markets has also shown me that international business is fundamentally about people. Conducting business in English, Russian, and Turkish—and now studying Chinese while living in China—has deepened my appreciation of the cross-cultural understanding, trust, and communication required to build lasting international partnerships.

Alongside my international business experience, I have developed a software development background that strengthens my ability to work with engineering-driven products and technology companies. This technical foundation has expanded my interest in advanced manufacturing sectors including automation, robotics, AI-enabled systems, EV components, IoT, and clean technology.

Today, based in China, I am particularly interested in the opportunities emerging across the China-EMEA-CIS corridor, where technology, manufacturing, and international business increasingly intersect.

If your company is preparing to enter new international markets, strengthen its international growth capabilities, or build a more structured approach to global expansion, let's start a conversation. I'd be pleased to exchange ideas and explore how I can contribute.

Insights

More than 20 years in international business have given me the opportunity to work across diverse markets, negotiate complex commercial agreements, build distributor networks, and help industrial manufacturers expand internationally. Along the way, I have gained practical perspectives that can only come from working through real business challenges and learning from their outcomes.

Sharing those experiences has become a natural extension of my professional work. Through workshops, export team training, guest lectures, conference presentations, and articles, I aim to translate practical experience into ideas, frameworks, and discussions that others can apply in their own international business environments.

Current topics include:

  • Cross-border negotiation in international business
  • International market expansion and distributor development
  • Building structured export operations
  • Trade risk management and commercial decision-making
  • Industrial technology and international business
  • Opportunities across the China-EMEA-CIS corridor

Whether you are organizing a conference, planning an internal workshop, delivering executive training, or inviting guest speakers for a university or business program, I would be pleased to discuss how I can contribute.
